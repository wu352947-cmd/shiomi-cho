# Credits — 汐見町 Shiomi-chō

All town geometry, materials, and textures are original and procedural (Blender Python + GLSL).
No ripped game assets and no real-world commercial brands appear in the scene.
Shop names (汐見食堂, かもめ書店, 潮風珈琲, etc.) are fictional.

## Inspiration (not assets)

Visual mood is inspired by Japanese seaside towns such as Onomichi, Manazuru, and Ine, and by the lighting and skies of Makoto Shinkai and Studio Ghibli films. No frames, models, or textures from those works were used.
